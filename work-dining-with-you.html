Dining with You…and Viruses

About the work

People often say to the friends and family around them, “Let’s have a meal together.” The point is usually not the meal but the “together.” Dining is a social situation unique to humans: sharing a table is how we grow closer. Yet with the outbreak of the pandemic, the shared table also became a social venue for germs and other microorganisms, prompting the public to reconsider, from the perspective of public health, the act of eating and the spaces and situations that come with it.

In today’s social climate, eating at a restaurant has become a bet with one’s life. In this online project, the artist goes on one-on-one online dates with participants. Through an interactive game designed by the artist, participants grow closer while experiencing the randomness and uncertainty of how germs may spread, and, together with the artist, gamble with each other’s lives without putting their lives at stake.

人們總對周圍的親朋好友說：「一起吃飯呀」，重點往往不是吃飯而是「一起」。用餐是人類獨有的社交情境，透過同桌共食增進情誼；然而隨著疫情爆發，同桌用膳也成了病菌等微生物的社交場所，也因而刺激大眾重新再次以公共衛生的角度，審慎思考看待「用餐進食」此一行為與其相應的空間情境。

在今時今日的社會情勢下，在餐廳用餐成了種攸關生命的賭注。此線上活動中，藝術家將與參與者進行一對一線上約會，透過藝術家所設計的互動遊戲，在增進友誼的同時體驗病菌傳播可能產生的隨機及不確定性，與藝術家一起在不會賭上性命的狀態下，賭上彼此的性命。
